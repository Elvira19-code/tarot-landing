# Card artwork

Historical Rider-Waite-Smith deck (1909), artwork by Pamela Colman Smith,
under the direction of Arthur Edward Waite.

JPEG source: https://github.com/sixseeds/tarot-api/tree/main/cards
The source repository identifies these images as public-domain scans of the
1909 deck: https://github.com/sixseeds/tarot-api#images

Background and version caveat:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Rider-Waite_tarot_deck
Modern recolorings and editions may have separate rights. Do not replace these
files with scans of a modern commercial edition without permission.

Files are served locally; selecting cards does not contact the source host.
ar00-ar21: major arcana; wa01-wa14: wands; cu01-cu14: cups;
sw01-sw14: swords; pe01-pe14: pentacles. Court ranks: page, knight, queen, king.
